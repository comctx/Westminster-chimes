Clock Chimes Plus
Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Clock Chimes Plus, previously named Westminster Chimes, is provided by Donald W. Reckman under DONCO-BAYCITY™.

Information collected by the app

The iPhone app does not collect or transmit personal information to us. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, tracking, or analytics services. We do not sell or share personal information collected through the app.

Settings stored on your device

Your selected sounds, chime schedules, quiet-hour schedules and names, and focus timer settings are stored locally on your device. These settings are used to operate the app and are not sent to our servers. Sounds are generated or included with the app.

Notifications and permissions

The app requests notification permission to play scheduled chimes while your phone is locked or the app is in the background. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device, without a remote notification server. You can change notification permission in your device Settings. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Your choices and deletion

You can change or remove quiet-hour schedules in the app, stop chimes using the Stop button, or turn off notifications in device Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that installation. Device backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.

Support email

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use this correspondence to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

This website and Apple services

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting the website involves your browser communicating with GitHub, whose handling of website traffic is governed by its own privacy statement. Apple separately handles App Store purchases, downloads, and any device diagnostics you choose to share under Apple's privacy practices. These are separate from the app's local settings and chime features.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through its features.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if our privacy practices change.
